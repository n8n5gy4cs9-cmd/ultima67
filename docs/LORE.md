# Lore & Content Bible

## Premise
Present day. The old gods were never gone: Yggdrasil is a lattice of **Bifrost gates** linking worlds. Corporations and militaries found the gates with satellites and modern weapons; the gods' remnants and Kalevala's singers wake up. You are a **Rune-Warden** (name your own) of **Midgård**, an Earth rebuilt after the gates opened: fjord cities, wired longhouses, runestones with antennas, assault rifles with runic sights.

## Main story (outline)
1. **Prologue, Midgård:** A Bifrost gate in your home town of *Kaupang* opens uncontrolled. A hooded skald warns of Fenrir's chains failing. You get your first runic pistol.
2. **Act I - Gather the Warband:** Recruit party (Valkyrie ex-soldier, dwarf gunsmith, Finnish singer-shaman, hacker-völva, bear-berserker). Find the **three Nornir threads** (Urðr, Verðandi, Skuld) in three Midgård regions. Learn spaceship sailing: the **Naglfar-II** longship.
3. **Act II - The Nine Realms (Sol):** Fenrir-Prime on Máni, forging the unbreakable chain **Gleipnir-2** at Dvalinn, Surtr's bunkers on Múspelheimr, alliance of the Vanir on Vanaheimr, Odin's vault on Ásgarðr, the dead bargain on Hel. Corporation **Aesir Dynamics** (mercenaries with modern guns) competes for relics.
4. **Act III - Ginnungagap:** The **Sampo-Gate** appears; Louhi has stolen the Sampo and is pulling Sol's light through it. Odin reveals the gods and Kalevala heroes were one old pact.
5. **Act IV - Kalevala:** Visit Väinölä (get *vainamoinen_gun*, the kantele-rifle), Tuonela (swan, get the dead's songs), Ilma, Pohjola, Sampola. Reforge the Sampo. Final: Louhi (+ Nidhögg) at Pohjola.
6. **Endings:** (a) Seal the gates, (b) Share the Sampo, (c) Take Odin's seat. Choices tracked by flags.

Kalevala: Kantele Temple, Swan River, Sky-Anvil, Iron Fortress, Sampo Shards x5.

## Missions
**Main:** the story above (~25 missions).
**Side (examples):** Retrieve Thor's hammer-drone; escort a skald to Valhalla karaoke; cannon defence of Kaupang harbour; hunt Ratatoskr (squirrel courier); repair the Bifrost relay; rescue miners; wolf-pack cull; brew mead for the Vanir festival; find 12 lost runestones.
**Optional tasks:** 100% rune collection, defeat Jörmungandr, fish all species, win Valhalla arena, build a longship fleet, secret ending, speedrun challenge.

## Naming guide
Use Old Norse names with diacritics in lore text but ASCII ids in code (`midgard`, `vainamoinen_gun`). Kalevala names Finnish ASCII ids (`vainola`, `pohjola`).
